## The story

**SnowBank** — a mid-size Australian retail bank.
Two stakeholders, **Sarah** (Head of Risk) and **Raj** (Data Platform Lead), have given us their input. Nothing else exists yet.

> In the next 30 minutes, **Cortex Code** will turn those notes into a working, governed analytics platform with a chat interface — and email a polished report from it.

No SQL written by hand. No tool-jumping. One workflow.
