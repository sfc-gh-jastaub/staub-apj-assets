# Notes from Sarah Chen — Head of Risk, SnowBank
*Subject: where we're at and what I need*
*Captured: informal call with consulting team*

---

Look, I'll be honest — risk reporting at SnowBank is held together with sticky tape. I've got a great team but they spend 80% of their week stitching CSVs together from three core banking systems and pivoting them in Excel before they can answer anything. By the time we get a number to the Exec Risk Committee it's already two weeks old.

The big things keeping me up at night:

- **Transaction monitoring.** Our current rules-based platform throws thousands of alerts a week and our analysts close 95% of them as false positives. We're missing real signal in the noise. I want to see customer-level patterns — *whose behaviour has changed?* — not just rule hits.
- **Segmenting risk properly.** Retail vs small business vs private wealth — they each have totally different baselines. Right now we treat them all the same and that's a problem.
- **The board keeps asking about anomalous activity** — international transfer spikes, structuring patterns, new high-risk merchants. I can't get clean answers fast.
- **Audit team has their own spreadsheet** of flagged customers that nobody else can see. We need that in one place.

What I want — honestly — is to be able to ask my data a question in plain English and get an answer I trust. *"Which private wealth customers have suddenly started moving money internationally that didn't before?"* I shouldn't need a SQL analyst to answer that.

If we can also get something the team can use day-to-day — a chat tool maybe — and have it email me a summary I can forward to the CRO without re-typing it… that's the dream.

Timeline-wise, I've got a board update in 6 weeks and I'd love to walk in with something working. Even if it's just one workstream done well.

— Sarah
